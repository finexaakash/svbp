About the college

Education with a bright future.

Sardar Vallabh Bhai Patel Mahavidyalaya Bhadra is in Hanumangarh, Rajasthan. The 2026–27 college flyer lists B.A. and M.A. study options.

The flyer lists B.A. subjects across humanities, social sciences and computer science, along with M.A. Geography and English Literature. Contact the college to confirm current course availability, subject combinations and admission details.
