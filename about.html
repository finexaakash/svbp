About the college

Knowledge with a classroom purpose.

Sardar Vallabh Bhai Patel B.Ed. College is located in Bhadra, Hanumangarh, Rajasthan, and focuses on teacher education.

B.Ed. teacher education prepares aspiring teachers to connect subject knowledge with the art and practice of teaching. Students explore how children learn, plan lessons, choose teaching strategies, assess progress and reflect on classroom experience. The college values curiosity, inclusion, clear communication and lifelong learning.
